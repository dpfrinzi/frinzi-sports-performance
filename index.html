Frequently Asked Questions

Is this only for football players?

No. Football and lineman development are specialties, but programs can be built for multi-sport athletes.

Do I need a full gym?

No. The program is designed around the equipment and space you have.

What if I have pain or a medical restriction?

Disclose it during the application process. Online coaching is not medical care. Appropriate medical clearance and restrictions must be followed.

What is the difference between Standard and Premium?

Premium adds priority messaging, more frequent adjustments and video-based technique feedback.

Can athletes under 18 enroll?

Yes, with parent or legal guardian involvement and approval.

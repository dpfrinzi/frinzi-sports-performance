FAQ

Questions before you start?

Is this only for football players?

No. Football and lineman development are specialties, but training can be built for multi-sport athletes and different experience levels.

Do I need a full gym?

No. Your program is built around the equipment and training space you report having access to.

What is the difference between Standard and Premium?

Standard includes the customized 12-week plan, training guidance, progress tracking and weekly check-in review. Premium adds priority messaging, more frequent adjustments when needed and video-based technique feedback.

Can athletes under 18 enroll?

Yes, with parent or legal guardian involvement and approval.

What if I have pain, an injury or a medical restriction?

Disclose it during the application and onboarding process and follow appropriate medical clearance or restrictions. Online coaching is educational performance guidance and is not medical evaluation, diagnosis or treatment.

Can I buy a package without applying first?

Yes. If you already know which coaching level you want, you can use the Standard or Premium checkout button. If you are unsure, start with the Game Plan application.

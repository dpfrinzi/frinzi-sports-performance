Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Frinzi Sports Performance provides customized coaching services that require planning and individualized work. This policy explains how cancellation and refund requests are handled.

Before customized coaching work begins

If you purchase a package and need to cancel before individualized program development or coaching services have begun, contact Frinzi Sports Performance as soon as possible. Refund requests will be reviewed based on the status of the service and any payment-processing limitations.

After program delivery or coaching begins

Once a customized program has been created, delivered, or coaching services have begun, payments are generally non-refundable because individualized work and service time have already been provided. This does not affect rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.

If Frinzi Sports Performance cannot provide the service

If Frinzi Sports Performance is unable to provide a material portion of a purchased service, an appropriate remedy may include rescheduling, replacement service, or a refund of the unprovided portion, depending on the circumstances.

Missed training or check-ins

Not completing workouts, check-ins, or messages does not automatically create a refund right. Athletes are encouraged to communicate schedule changes or barriers so the coaching plan can be adjusted when appropriate.

Technology and third-party platforms

Temporary issues involving Stripe, Google Forms, internet service, or a coaching platform do not automatically cancel a coaching package. Reasonable efforts will be made to restore access or provide an alternative method when needed.

How to request help

Email dpfrinzi@yahoo.com with the purchaser's name, package purchased, purchase date, and the reason for the request.

This policy is intended to explain current business practices clearly and does not limit rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Frinzi Sports Performance, operated by Frinzi Performance LLC, handles information submitted through this website and related coaching services.

Information we may collect

Information may include your name, email address, athlete age, sport, position, goals, training history, schedule, equipment access, and other information you voluntarily provide through an application, onboarding form, check-in, email, or coaching platform.

How information is used

Information is used to review coaching applications, communicate with athletes or parents/guardians, build and manage training programs, provide support, process purchases, maintain records, and improve the coaching service.

Payments

Payments are processed through Stripe. Frinzi Sports Performance does not directly store full payment-card numbers submitted through Stripe checkout. Stripe's own privacy and security practices apply to information processed through its service.

Forms and coaching platforms

The business may use third-party services such as Google Forms and a training/coaching application to collect onboarding information, deliver programs, track progress, or communicate. Information submitted through those services is also subject to the applicable provider's terms and privacy practices.

Minors

A parent or legal guardian must be involved in enrollment for athletes under 18. Parents or guardians should avoid submitting information that is not reasonably necessary for coaching.

Sharing

Personal information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the business, process payments, deliver coaching, maintain technology, or comply with law. Frinzi Sports Performance does not intentionally publish private athlete information without appropriate permission.

Data retention and security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect business records and limit access to information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained for business, tax, legal, safety, or recordkeeping purposes as reasonably necessary.

Your choices

You may contact Frinzi Sports Performance to ask about correcting or deleting information you previously submitted, subject to any records that must be retained for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to dpfrinzi@yahoo.com.

This policy is intended to provide a clear description of current business practices and does not limit rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.
